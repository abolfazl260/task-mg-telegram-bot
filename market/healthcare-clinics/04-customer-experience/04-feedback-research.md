# تحقیق مشتری و Feedback — Healthcare Clinics

> وضعیت سند: Research System v1  
> هدف: تبدیل مصاحبه، Pilot، Support، Lost Deal و Churn به سیستم تصمیم‌گیری برای Product/GTM؛ نه مجموعه‌ای از نظرهای پراکنده.

---

# 1. Research Principles

1. Problem before solution.
2. Observe behavior, not only opinions.
3. Separate user from buyer.
4. Capture evidence consistently.
5. Do not promise features during research.
6. Distinguish signal from one-off request.
7. Protect patient/customer sensitive data.
8. Close the loop with Product and GTM.

---

# 2. Research Questions

در هر مرحله، سؤال تحقیق متفاوت است.

## Market Validation

- آیا Pain واقعی است؟
- چند بار رخ می‌دهد؟
- چه کسی بیشترین درد را دارد؟
- workaround فعلی چیست؟

## Product Validation

- آیا workflow قابل استفاده است؟
- friction کجاست؟
- آیا Next Action model طبیعی است؟

## GTM Validation

- چه messageای resonate می‌کند؟
- buyer چگونه خرید می‌کند؟
- price sensitivity چیست؟

## Retention Validation

- چرا product بخشی از habit شده یا نشده؟
- چه چیزی churn risk می‌سازد؟

# 6. Participant Selection

Avoid interviewing only friendly users.

Sample should include:

- small clinics
- medium clinics
- high workflow complexity
- low complexity
- current tool users
- manual process users
- won customers
- lost prospects

---

# 7. Interview Recruitment

Recruit through:

- active pipeline
- customers
- referrals
- professional partners
- associations

Participation incentive اگر لازم است، باید transparent باشد و پاسخ را خریداری نکند.

# 8. Discovery Interview

هدف:
فهم رفتار فعلی.

## Start

«آخرین باری که یک follow-up یا کار داخلی عقب افتاد چه اتفاقی افتاد؟»

## Timeline

- trigger
- person
- tool
- handoff
- delay
- outcome

## Avoid

«آیا دوست دارید AI این را حل کند؟»

این سؤال leading است.

---

# 9. Interview Questions — Owner

- برای فهمیدن وضعیت عملیات چه می‌کنید؟
- آخرین failure مهم چه بود؟
- کدام فرد اگر نباشد process آسیب می‌بیند؟
- چه metricای آرزو دارید ببینید؟
- چه زمانی نرم‌افزار جدید ارزش خرید دارد؟

---

# 10. Interview Questions — Manager

- صبح از کجا می‌فهمید چه کارهایی باز است؟
- overdue را چگونه پیدا می‌کنید؟
- چه چیزهایی بیشترین follow-up دستی می‌خواهد؟
- بین شیفت‌ها چه چیزی گم می‌شود؟
- هفته گذشته چه bottleneckی داشتید؟

---

# 11. Interview Questions — Reception

- چیزی که باید بعداً انجام دهید کجا ثبت می‌کنید؟
- چند کانال را هم‌زمان چک می‌کنید؟
- چه چیزی بیشتر فراموش می‌شود؟
- چه چیزی در نرم‌افزار فعلی کند است؟
- اگر یک ابزار عالی بود، چه چیزی را سریع‌تر می‌کرد؟

---

# 12. Interview Questions — Doctor

- چه کارهایی را به staff واگذار می‌کنید؟
- چه چیزهایی دوباره برای status به شما برمی‌گردد؟
- چه نوع notification آزاردهنده است؟
- چه interactionی را حاضر نیستید انجام دهید؟
- کجا admin work زمان بالینی را می‌گیرد؟

---

# 15. Shadowing

Shadowing یعنی مشاهده واقعی workflow.

## Observe

- interruptions
- tools
- handoffs
- sticky notes
- chat
- duplicate entry
- waiting
- escalation

هر inefficiency را همان لحظه solution ندهیم.

underlying job.

- severity
- ICP relevance

# 26. Evidence Levels

## Level 1
One opinion.

## Level 2
Repeated interview.

## Level 3
Observed behavior.

## Level 4
Usage data.

## Level 5
Commercial evidence:
customer pays/leaves because of it.

Priority should increase with evidence.

---

# 27. Lost Deal Analysis

Every meaningful lost deal should have:

- segment
- stage lost
- primary reason
- competitor/alternative
- missing capability
- pricing
- decision process
- re-open trigger

---

# 28. Lost Deal Interview

If relationship allows:

- what did you choose?
- what was decisive?
- what risk did you see?
- where did our process/product fail?
- what would have changed decision?

No defensive selling.

---

# 29. Competitor Insight

Lost deal data is better than assumptions.

Track:

- competitor
- reason
- perceived advantage
- price if voluntarily known
- integration
- trust
- workflow fit

---

# 30. Churn Interview

Timing:

close to cancellation, but after immediate operational offboarding is clear.

Questions:

- when did value start declining?
- what changed?
- what did you replace it with?
- primary reason?
- what could realistically have prevented churn?

---

# 31. Churn Evidence

Separate:

- product
- adoption
- relationship
- price
- business change

Do not label everything "price" because customer said expensive.

---

# 32. Pricing Research

Use:

- willingness-to-pay interviews
- proposal acceptance
- discount requests
- lost reason
- package choice

Stated WTP only one signal.

Actual purchase > stated preference.

# 35. Survey Strategy

Survey useful for:

- ranking
- segmentation
- quantitative follow-up

Not good for deep problem discovery alone.

Interview first, survey after themes.

---

# 36. In-Product Feedback

Lightweight:

- issue report
- feedback
- thumbs up/down on AI
- reason on failed workflow

Avoid constant popups.

---

# 37. Support Mining

Monthly mine support tickets:

- top issue
- repeated terminology confusion
- broken workflow
- training gap
- feature request

Support is continuous research source.

---

# 38. Search/Usage Mining

Look at:

- common searches
- empty results
- filters used
- unused features
- repeated navigation

Behavior shows mental model.

---

# 39. AI Feedback

For AI actions capture:

- accepted
- edited
- rejected
- reason
- correction

This becomes evaluation data.

Sensitive content must be handled safely.

---

# 40. Research Privacy

Never copy unnecessary patient-identifying data into research notes.

Use:

- anonymized examples
- synthetic cases
- redaction

Consent for recording/interview according to applicable requirements.

---

# 41. Research Recording

If calls recorded:

- participant informed
- storage controlled
- retention defined
- access limited

---

# 42. Research Repository Access

Not everyone needs raw customer data.

Access:

- product/research team
- limited internal roles

Summaries can be broader.

---

# 43. Insight Format

Good insight:

**Managers in 4/5 observed clinics keep a separate manual callback list because PMS appointment records do not represent next action ownership.**

Bad insight:

**Customers want better workflow.**

Insight should include evidence and context.

---

# 44. Opportunity Statement

Template:

**[Persona] struggles to [job] when [situation], because [root cause], leading to [impact].**

# 46. Insight Prioritization

# 50. Research Anti-Patterns

- asking only current customers
- leading questions
- feature voting
- over-weighting loud customer
- mixing sales pitch into interview
- ignoring negative evidence
- collecting notes without synthesis

# 54. Final Research Principle

Research باید از «مشتری چه featureای خواست؟» به این سؤال برسد:

**چه Jobی تکرار می‌شود، چه Painی measurable است، چه behaviorای مشاهده شده و چه تصمیمی باید تغییر کند؟**
